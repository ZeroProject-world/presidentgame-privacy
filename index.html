Privacy Policy for 总统治国 (President Game)

Last updated: November 14, 2026

总统治国 is a governance simulation game: choose a president, govern a country for 8 years, and answer one question per quarter. This policy explains what data the app collects. In short: none.

Data Collection

总统治国 does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no advertisements, no analytics, no internet connection, and no third-party SDKs in version 1.0.

On-Device Data Only

Your game progress — best grade achieved — is stored only on your device and never leaves it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Children's Privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children under 13 either.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated in the future, for example when new features are added. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us at jrealty2023@gmail.com.

最后更新：2026年11月14日
