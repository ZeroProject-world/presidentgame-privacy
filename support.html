总统治国 (President Game) — Support

How to play

Pick a president, then pick a country. Each quarter you face one national question with four options. Your choices move four indicators: Economy, People, Environment, and Military. If any indicator hits 0 or 100, your term ends early. Survive all 8 years (32 quarters) to finish your term and earn a grade.

Language

Tap EN / 中文 in the game to switch between English and Chinese. The default is English.

No sound?

Make sure your phone volume is turned up. The game plays sound even when the silent switch is on.

Contact

Questions, feedback, or bug reports? Email jrealty2023@gmail.com.

支持（中文）

玩法：选一位总统，再选一个国家。每季度遇到一道国事题，四个选项。你的选择会改变经济、民心、环境、军力四项指标；任一指标归零或冲满 100 都会提前下台。撑满 8 年（32 个季度）卸任并获得评级。

语言：点游戏里的 EN / 中文 按钮切换中英文，默认英文。没有声音？把手机音量调大即可，静音开关开着也有声音。问题、建议或 bug 请发邮件至 jrealty2023@gmail.com。
